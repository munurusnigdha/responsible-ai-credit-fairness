Research Findings

Based on the single 80/20 stratified train/test split

1
Logistic Regression performed slightly better.
Accuracy: 78% versus 76%; ROC AUC: 0.804 versus 0.784.
2
Both models predicted higher risk for younger applicants.
Under-35 predicted rates were 31.4% and 26.7%, compared with 15.8% and 10.5% for ages 35+.
3
False positive rates differed across age groups.
This motivates further fairness investigation, but does not establish discrimination.

Research Design & Limitations

Interpretation matters as much as model accuracy

↗
Dataset: UCI Statlog (German Credit), 1,000 records, 20 features; historical and not representative of current lending.
↗
Method: Logistic Regression and Random Forest; stratified 80/20 split; preprocessing fit on training data.
↗
Caution: Small age-group samples, arbitrary exploratory age cutoff, single split, no uncertainty intervals, and no causal or legal discrimination claim.
