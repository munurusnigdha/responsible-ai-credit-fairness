What the analysis tells us

Interpretation and limitations

01 / PERFORMANCE

A simple baseline remained competitive

Logistic regression reached 78% accuracy and 0.804 ROC AUC; random forest reached 76% and 0.784 on the same test split.

02 / GROUP DIFFERENCES

Predictions varied by age group

Both models flagged a larger share of applicants under 35 as high risk. The observed high-risk rates also differed between groups.

03 / RESPONSIBLE USE

Fairness requires more than one metric

This historical 1,000-record dataset and one train/test split cannot establish present-day lending fairness or causal discrimination.
