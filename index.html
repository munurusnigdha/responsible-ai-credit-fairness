COMPLETED EXPLORATORY ANALYSIS · REAL DATA

Causal Evaluation of Email Marketing Effectiveness

Research question: Do randomized email campaigns increase customer conversion and revenue compared with sending no email?

Dataset: Hillstrom email marketing experiment (64,000 customers).

Findings: Men's email increased conversion by 0.681 percentage points and average revenue by $0.77 per customer; women's email increased conversion by 0.311 percentage points and average revenue by $0.42 per customer, each compared with no email. Both conversion comparisons were statistically significant.

Methods: Randomized A/B test analysis, two-proportion tests, confidence intervals, revenue comparisons.

COMPLETED EXPLORATORY ANALYSIS · US PUBLIC DATA

US Mortgage Lending & Responsible AI

Research question: Can machine learning predict historical mortgage denials, and how do prediction errors vary across demographic groups?

Dataset: 2023 Massachusetts HMDA: 210,643 reported records; 149,649 eligible applications.

Methods: Majority baseline, Logistic Regression, Random Forest and Gradient Boosting; stratified 80/20 split; demographic fairness audit and runtime measurement.

Results: Gradient Boosting achieved 81.37% accuracy and 0.798 ROC-AUC on 29,930 test cases, but identified only 28.44% of denials at the default threshold. This illustrates why accuracy alone is insufficient. Demographic disparities are descriptive, not evidence of discrimination.
